## About Me

I work full-time in telecommunications infrastructure operations while studying part-time for an **Associate Degree in Data Analytics**(Online) through the University of Adelaide. My background spans electronic and communications engineering, network operations, and cybersecurity - I learn best by building things in a homelab before touching anything important.

When I'm not on shift, I'm running self-hosted services on my HP ProDesk 600 G6 Mini PC (Fedora Server, Docker), experimenting with local LLMs on my MacBook Pro M4 Pro, and documenting what I learn here.
